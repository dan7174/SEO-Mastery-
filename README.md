## Recommended Operating Sequence

A successful local SEO engagement begins with verified business facts—not pages. Finalize the Google Business Profile category/service inventory, NAP information, service areas, proof points, customer reviews, and commercial priorities. Then create the Core 30 architecture, build the site structure, develop the conversion-led GBP landing page, complete category and service pages, add authentic About/Contact evidence, and perform technical/indexability validation.

After the foundation is live, use rank-grid data to identify geographic opportunity areas. Prioritize “striking-distance” areas where the business ranks in positions 4–6, but only publish location-oriented pages when research produces distinct, relevant local information. Do not create interchangeable city-name variants.

## Guardrails

The Core 30 system should never manufacture trust. Verify claims about review counts, licenses, turnaround times, guarantees, availability, ownership, and project history before publication. Human review remains required for real-person content, sensitive customer-facing communication, claim-heavy sales copy, and production releases.

Do not rely on generic content, keyword stuffing, or black-box ranking promises. Explain the scope of work, required client inputs, measurement approach, and next actions. Measure the system through rank grids, indexation, GBP performance, qualified leads, conversion paths, and technical health rather than a single ranking metric.
